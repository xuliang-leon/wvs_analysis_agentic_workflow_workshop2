How do values differ across five Asian countries?

Four views of a simulated survey: where each country sits on the map of values, what makes each one distinctive, how much individuals overlap, and how money relates to happiness.

This data is synthetic. It is simulated, fabricated to resemble the World Values Survey, Wave 7, the model it is based on. No real respondents are included, so every finding here is illustrative only. Countries: Turkey (Middle East), India (South Asia), Singapore (Southeast Asia), China (East Asia) and Kazakhstan (Central Asia). Model survey citation: Haerpfer, C., Inglehart, R., Moreno, A., Welzel, C., Kizilova, K., Diez-Medrano J., Lagos, M., Norris, P., Ponarin, E. & Puranen, B. (eds.). 2022. World Values Survey: Round Seven – Country-Pooled Datafile Version 6.0. Madrid, Spain & Vienna, Austria: JD Systems Institute & WVSA Secretariat. worldvaluessurvey.org
